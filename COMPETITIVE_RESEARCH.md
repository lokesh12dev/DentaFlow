## Part 2 — Verified regulatory posture (do not copy claims)

- Only Overjet (12), Pearl (8), Videa (4), Diagnocat (2, narrow), Denti.AI (2) hold **verifiable public FDA 510(k) letters** for radiograph AI. **No radiograph-AI in our list holds a published CDSCO (India) registration** — scanO's own blog says a US clearance "does not automatically establish regulatory permission in India". DentalFlow must therefore **never** present an LLM as a diagnostic device; clinical-AI output stays review-only with provenance (already enforced by our Edge-Function guardrails).
- India compliance stack for a PMS: DPDPA (consent, purpose limitation), GST invoicing (CGST/SGST/HSN-SAC), TRAI-DLT templates for SMS/WhatsApp, and ABDM/ABHA readiness where the clinic opts in. BestoSys has GST+WhatsApp today; ABDM is openly "on roadmap" even for the market leader — an open gap for DentalFlow [T/V mix].

## Part 3 — Opportunities (each labeled by evidence status)

1. **Price honesty + India price band.** [Evidence: 8 of 12 cannot be bought without a sales call; the Indian market's leader publishes ₹1,149–2,475/mo.] DentalFlow ships self-serve pricing in the Indian band with no long contracts. *Hypothesis to validate:* clinics' willingness to pay a premium for AI/analytics over BestoSys-tier products.
2. **Connection-tolerant operations.** [Evidence: Curve and BestoSys are cloud-only with no usable offline mode — their own weak points as reported.] DentalFlow's SQLite+SQLCipher local layer already queues permitted non-PHI mutations; frame honestly ("offline queue for non-sensitive actions; clinical data requires connectivity"), never claim full offline PHI.
3. **Analytics with formulas on screen.** [Evidence: vendor-reported metrics ($44K/mo opportunities, 25% acceptance lift) are marketing, unsourced.] Our `buildClinicAnalytics` renders every metric's definition in-product — a small trust feature none of the 12 offers.
4. **Multi-branch at unlimited seats.** [Evidence: CareStack charges per location from $829; scanO advertises unlimited staff; BestoSys unlimited users.] DentalFlow's clinic/branch/member model with RLS is built per-seat-free; pricing must keep it that way.
5. **UPI/GST-native billing.** [Evidence: every Indian tool has GST; none of the US tools do; UPI QR/razorpay webhooks are unspecified or absent in competitor materials.] Schema already has payment_method='upi' and invoice tax fields — extend to GST breakdown + HSN codes + UPI receipt flows. *Validate*: proportion of clinics needing insurance/CGHS workflows vs pure cash-pay.
6. **Recall + WhatsApp DLT compliance as a first-class flow** (BestoSys charges ₹3,960/yr for WhatsApp; scanO sells message-linked revenue recovery). DentalFlow's outbox + template approval model maps directly to DLT template rules.
7. **Clinician-in-command AI.** All AI competitors cleared as "second reader"; we adopt the same posture: review-required, source-links, no autonomous diagnosis — which for India-first launches is also the safest regulatory stance.
8. **Non-goals repeated from audit:** no radiograph-AI of our own (partner or ignore), no orthodontic remote monitoring, no US insurance RCM until post-India.

## Part 4 — Unmet needs worth validating with real dentists (interview topics, not claims)

1. Chairside tooth-chart speed on a phone/tablet vs desktop chart layouts (current quality of our ToothChart.tsx untested with users).
2. Wi-Fi reliability in Indian clinics → what must actually work without internet (billing capture? booking?).
3. Expected price point for the 1–3 chair Tamil Nadu clinic (BestoSys band vs scanO band).
4. WhatsApp confirm/cancel loops: is two-way booking reconciliation more valuable than reminders?
5. Reporting the owner actually reads: collections + chair utilisation + recall backlog — do our definitions match their mental model?
6. AI trust: will staff accept a note-drafting assistant with mandatory review? (ScanO's per-interaction pricing may be the expected pattern.)
7. Duplicate patient identity control: does name+normalized-phone matching suffice, and when does UPI/VPA (ABDMA) linking matter?
